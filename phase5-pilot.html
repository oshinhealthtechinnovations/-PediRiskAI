Phase V Roadmap

Phase V – Pilot Study & Clinical Validation

Real-world clinical pilot across multi-center schools and clinics, ethics compliance, statistical accuracy benchmarking, and peer-reviewed journal publishing.

CLINICAL TRIAL BLUEPRINT

Phase V Implementation Plan

1. Ethics & Institutional Review (IRB)

  • Obtain formal Institutional Ethics Committee clearance.
  • Execute digital parent consent and school permission protocols.

2. Multi-Site Recruitment & Setup

  • Partner with representative primary schools and pediatric hospitals.
  • Train school health staff on digital questionnaire deployment.

3. AI Accuracy & Clinical Audit

  • Compare AI risk predictions against blinded doctor diagnoses and lab tests (HbA1c/FPG).
  • Audit false positives and false negatives to refine prediction cut-offs.

4. Statistical Analysis & Publishing

  • Calculate ROC-AUC, Sensitivity, Specificity, PPV, and NPV with 95% CIs.
  • Publish validation findings in peer-reviewed medical journals.

PROJECT FOUNDATION & PURPOSE

Need of the Project & Objectives

Understanding the clinical necessity and primary goals behind the PediRisk AI research framework.

1. Need of the Project

Pediatric Type 2 Diabetes (T2D) and early metabolic syndrome have emerged as major public health threats in children and adolescents. The critical need for PediRisk AI arises from four major clinical challenges:

  • Silent & Rapid Disease Progression: Unlike adult diabetes, pediatric T2D progresses rapidly, causing irreversible beta-cell loss before symptoms appear.
  • Invasive Blood Draw Barriers: Routine blood tests (HbA1c, OGTT) cause severe needle anxiety in young children, leading to low screening compliance in schools.
  • High Costs of Mass Screening: Large-scale blood phlebotomy programs in public schools are financially prohibitive and logistically unfeasible.
  • Lack of Early Non-Invasive Tools: Current screening relies solely on basic BMI, which fails to capture central adiposity or clinical skin markers like Acanthosis Nigricans.

2. Aim & Objectives

Primary Aim: To develop, validate, and deploy an explainable machine learning platform for 100% non-invasive early risk stratification of pediatric Type 2 Diabetes.

Core Project Objectives:

  • Objective 1: Non-Invasive Data Protocol Identify and validate key physical predictors (Waist-to-Height Ratio, BMI percentiles, BP percentiles, skin markers, and family history).
  • Objective 2: Explainable AI Model Development Train gradient boosting models (XGBoost/LightGBM) integrated with SHAP/LIME for full physician attribution transparency.
  • Objective 3: Web-Based Calculator Platform Deploy an interactive, secure Web application for pediatricians and parents with automated risk scoring and intervention guides.
  • Objective 4: Clinical Pilot & Sensitivity Audit Conduct multi-center hospital pilot trials to achieve ≥88% clinical sensitivity against gold-standard lab tests.
PROJECT OVERVIEW

About the PediRisk AI Project

Addressing the global healthcare challenge of underdiagnosed childhood metabolic risk through ethical, explainable artificial intelligence.

The Clinical Necessity for Early Screening

Pediatric Type 2 Diabetes (T2D) and metabolic syndrome have seen a dramatic rise globally due to shifts in dietary patterns, sedentary lifestyles, and childhood obesity. Unlike adult diabetes, T2D in children progresses rapidly and aggressively, often remaining asymptomatic until severe vascular or microvascular complications develop.

Traditional diagnostic methods rely on invasive phlebotomy (fasting blood glucose, oral glucose tolerance tests, and HbA1c). In school-based mass screenings and routine pediatric wellness visits, blood draws cause significant anxiety, low compliance, and high operational costs.

The PediRisk AI Innovation

PediRisk AI solves this challenge by leveraging 100% non-invasive data points — including anthropometrics (Waist-to-Height Ratio, BMI percentiles), parental metabolic history, dietary habits, skin markers (Acanthosis Nigricans), and physical activity levels — to stratify risk before blood draws are required.

Silent Progression

Childhood T2D progresses up to 4x faster than adult T2D, requiring early intervention prior to clinical onset.

Child-Friendly

Zero needle pricks or blood draws during initial risk stratification in school screening drives.

Physician Trust

Generates SHAP feature attribution charts explaining every risk score to pediatricians.

IRB & Ethics

Built adhering to clinical trial ethics, parental consent protocols, and privacy encryption.

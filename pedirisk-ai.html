THE HEALTHCARE CHALLENGE

Why PediRisk AI Matters

Addressing the global surge in childhood obesity and underdiagnosed pediatric Type 2 Diabetes.

Underdiagnosed T2D Risk

Pediatric Type 2 Diabetes often progresses silently without early symptoms, leading to microvascular complications before clinical detection.

Invasive Blood Barriers

Routine blood draws for fasting blood glucose and HbA1c cause anxiety in young children, leading to low screening participation in schools.

Non-Invasive AI Prediction

PediRisk AI evaluates physical anthropometrics, clinical history, lifestyle habits, and non-invasive physiological variables to stratification risk early.

TRANSPARENT AI

Explainable AI (XAI) for Clinical Trust

PediRisk AI rejects "black-box" machine learning. Every risk score prediction comes with clinical feature attribution explanations powered by SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) and LIME.

Physician Attribution Summary

Highlights exactly which factors (e.g. BMI percentile, family history, waist-to-height ratio) contributed to the risk score.

Actionable Guidance for Parents

Generates personalized dietary and lifestyle intervention recommendations based on identified risk factors.

Key Predictive Variables

  • Waist-to-Height Ratio (WtHR)
  • BMI-for-Age Percentiles
  • Maternal & Paternal T2D History
  • Physical Activity & Screen Time Hours
  • Acanthosis Nigricans Skin Observations
  • Blood Pressure Percentiles

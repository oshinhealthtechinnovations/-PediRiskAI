PediRisk AI: Non-Invasive Pediatric T2D Risk Assessment Platform

A Machine Learning & Explainable AI (XAI) Framework for Early Risk Identification of Pediatric Type 2 Diabetes and Metabolic Syndrome in Children & Adolescents.

View Clinical Necessity Research Objectives
CLINICAL NECESSITY

1. Need of the Project

Pediatric Type 2 Diabetes (T2D) is an escalating public health crisis. The PediRisk AI framework directly solves four critical healthcare challenges:

HIGH URGENCY

Silent & Rapid Progression

Unlike adult diabetes, pediatric T2D progresses rapidly, causing irreversible pancreatic beta-cell destruction before overt symptoms manifest.

SCREENING BARRIER

Invasive Blood Draw Anxiety

Routine diagnostic blood draws (HbA1c, OGTT) cause severe phlebotomy fear in children, resulting in extremely low screening compliance in schools.

COST IMPACT

High Mass Screening Costs

Large-scale laboratory phlebotomy screening in public school districts is financially prohibitive and logistically unmanageable for healthcare systems.

METHODOLOGY GAP

Lack of Non-Invasive Tools

Existing screening relies on standalone BMI percentiles, failing to detect central adiposity, blood pressure trends, or Acanthosis Nigricans skin markers.

RESEARCH TARGETS

2. Aim & Core Objectives

Primary Aim: To build, validate, and deploy an explainable machine learning platform for 100% non-invasive pediatric risk stratification.

01
PROTOCOL VALIDATED

Non-Invasive Predictor Protocol

Define and validate key physical non-invasive parameters: Waist-to-Height Ratio (WtHR), BMI percentiles, blood pressure percentiles, skin markers, and maternal/paternal history.

02
XAI ENGINE BUILT

Explainable AI Model Engine

Train gradient boosting models (XGBoost & LightGBM) integrated with SHAP and LIME algorithms to provide transparent feature attribution for pediatricians.

03
WEB CALCULATOR READY

Web Risk Calculator Platform

Develop an interactive, secure web-based application enabling pediatricians and parents to calculate risk scores with automated intervention guides.

04
CLINICAL PILOT

Clinical Pilot & Sensitivity Audit

Execute multi-center hospital pilot trials to achieve ≥88% clinical sensitivity against gold-standard lab tests (HbA1c & Fasting Glucose).

PHYSICIAN TRANSPARENCY

SHAP Feature Attribution Model

PediRisk AI rejects "black-box" predictions. Every pediatric risk score generated provides exact SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) attribution weights so physicians know precisely why a child is categorized as high or moderate risk.

  • Physician Attribution Breakdown: Shows exact percentage impact of each non-invasive physical marker.
  • Actionable Parent Interventions: Generates targeted lifestyle and dietary guidance tailored to the highest-weight risk factors.

Model Feature Weights XGBoost SHAP

Waist-to-Height Ratio (WtHR) 35% Impact
BMI-for-Age Percentiles 25% Impact
Maternal & Paternal T2D History 20% Impact
Blood Pressure & Skin Markers 12% Impact
Physical Activity & Screen Hours 8% Impact

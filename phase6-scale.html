Phase VI Roadmap

Phase VI – Deployment & Scale-Up

National implementation strategy, institutional capacity building, AI drift monitoring, commercialization, and expansion to comprehensive pediatric metabolic health conditions.

COMMERCIAL & SCALING BLUEPRINT

Phase VI Implementation Plan

1. Distribution & Public Health Rollout

  • Roll out to school networks, primary health centres (PHCs), and NGOs.
  • Integrate with government health program initiatives.

2. Capacity Building & User Training

  • Conduct doctor and school nurse training workshops.
  • Provide user manuals and 24/7 technical support desk.

3. AI Drift & System Performance Monitoring

  • Implement continuous model drift monitoring and population recalibration.
  • Establish quarterly quality assurance audits.

4. Platform Vision Extension

  • Commercialize via school subscriptions, hospital licensing, and CSR programs.
  • Extend AI framework to Childhood Obesity, Pediatric Hypertension, NAFLD, and PCOS.

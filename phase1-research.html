Phase I Roadmap

Phase I – Research Foundation

Establishing the epidemiological background, clinical literature taxonomy, risk variable taxonomy, and initial screening questionnaires for pediatric Type 2 Diabetes.

EXECUTION BLUEPRINT

Phase I Implementation Plan

A structured step-by-step roadmap to execute Phase I deliverables effectively.

1. Problem & Gap Analysis

  • Collect regional and global epidemiological data on rising childhood obesity and T2D.
  • Identify diagnostic bottlenecks in traditional clinical blood testing.
  • Establish clear cost-benefit metrics for digital pre-screening tools.

2. Systematic Literature Review

  • Audit ADA, ISPAD, and Indian Academy of Pediatrics (IAP) clinical guidelines.
  • Synthesize existing pediatric risk score calculators and AI screening papers.
  • Document missing research gaps in non-invasive early detection.

3. Clinical Variable Taxonomy

  • Catalog demographic indicators (Age, Sex, Ethnicity, Socioeconomic status).
  • Select anthropometric indicators (BMI percentile, Waist-to-Height ratio).
  • Define lifestyle parameters (Dietary patterns, Physical activity, Screen time).

4. Questionnaire V1 Drafting

  • Draft Parent Version for household lifestyle & family history.
  • Draft School Version for mass checkups and physical education records.
  • Draft Doctor Version for clinical triage and physical signs.

About PediRisk AI

Revolutionizing Early Detection of Childhood Type 2 Diabetes Through Explainable Artificial Intelligence

PediRisk AI is designed to support the early identification of Type 2 Diabetes risk among children and adolescents through an evidence-based, non-invasive, AI-powered screening platform.

The project addresses a significant gap in pediatric healthcare by providing a digital pre-screening tool that evaluates multiple risk factors before the onset of clinical symptoms. Rather than functioning as a diagnostic system, PediRisk AI serves as an intelligent clinical decision support and triage platform.

THE URGENCY

Why PediRisk AI?

Early Identification

Catches high-risk pediatric profiles before clinical complications manifest.

Non-Invasive Screening

Digital health screening without needle pricks or immediate laboratory delays.

AI Decision Support

Empowers pediatricians with transparent risk stratification and triage tools.

Explainable & Transparent

Clear, interpretable risk factor breakdowns (SHAP/LIME feature rankings).

School-Based Screening

Seamlessly integrates into mass school health checkups and community initiatives.

Population Monitoring

Provides public health authorities with aggregate epidemiological insights.

PROJECT GOALS

PediRisk AI Objectives

01

Evidence-Based Questionnaire

Develop standardized pediatric screening forms for parents, schools, and doctors.

02

Identify Risk Variables

Pinpoint clinically significant demographics, lifestyle, anthropometric, and clinical indicators.

03

Build Predictive Models

Train state-of-the-art ML models (XGBoost, Random Forest, LightGBM, Ensemble Learning).

04

Implement Explainable AI

Provide transparent decision support showing why a child is classified into specific risk tiers.

Phase II Roadmap

Phase II – Clinical Scientific Consultation

Multi-disciplinary expert review, clinical variable validation, risk scoring framework formulation, and referral pathway protocol definition.

CLINICAL PROTOCOL BLUEPRINT

Phase II Implementation Plan

1. Expert Advisory Panel Setup

  • Form multi-specialty board (Pediatricians, Endocrinologists, Diabetologists, Epidemiologists).
  • Schedule structured Delphi rounds to review candidate risk variables.

2. Clinical Logic & Variable Pruning

  • Eliminate subjective or low-sensitivity variables to optimize response speed.
  • Standardize response formats (binary, numerical, ordinal scales).

3. Risk Weightage & Scoring Framework

  • Establish weighted coefficient bounds for physical signs and family history.
  • Define risk tiers (Low Risk, Moderate Risk, High Risk) with actionable cut-offs.

4. Clinical Referral Protocol

  • Build referral decision pathways connecting school screenings to pediatric clinics.
  • Finalize Questionnaire V2 & clinical validation report.

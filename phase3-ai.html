Phase III Roadmap

Phase III – AI Scientific Consultation

Designing data strategy, feature engineering pipelines, predictive machine learning algorithms, and explainable AI (SHAP & LIME) decision frameworks.

AI & DATA SCIENCE BLUEPRINT

Phase III Implementation Plan

1. Data Governance & Inclusion Strategy

  • Define data ingestion standards and anonymization pipelines.
  • Establish strict inclusion/exclusion clinical criteria for training data.

2. Feature Engineering & Selection

  • Construct derived clinical variables (e.g., Waist-Height ratio, risk interaction scores).
  • Perform feature importance ranking using Random Forest and Lasso.

3. Machine Learning Model Training

  • Benchmark XGBoost, LightGBM, Random Forest, Logistic Regression, and Neural Networks.
  • Perform k-fold cross-validation and hyperparameter optimization.

4. Explainable AI (XAI) Framework

  • Integrate SHAP (Shapley Additive exPlanations) for global feature importance.
  • Implement LIME for individualized patient-level risk factor explanations.
